Funding Pulse · Digest & Dashboard — Components Map

The problem

Aarav, a venture scout at Cloudburst Ventures (a Bengaluru family office), has no quick, trustworthy weekly view of Indian startup funding, or of how it touches the companies the fund backs and watches.

What Funding Pulse does

Collects the week's funding news, pulls out each deal, matches it to the fund's 156 Portfolio and 25 On Radar companies, works out every number in code, explains the numbers with source links, and emails a short digest every Monday at 06:00 IST. A public dashboard (Brief, Market, Actions) lets Aarav dig in, ask questions and send drafts.

Boundaries

The AI never does the maths · every claim links to a source · nothing is invented · no social media or LinkedIn scraping · nothing goes to a founder unless Aarav confirms · no login to read

Edge cases handled (red tags on the map)

E1 News, last 7 days
What can go wrong: A news source cannot be read (for example 6 of 13 on a recent run), or a search provider runs out
What happens: The outlet is searched instead through a chain of four providers (24-hour cache, 30 searches a run). The data strip names the sources that were unreachable.
E2 Collect and clean
What can go wrong: An article contains instructions meant for the AI, or is a social post
What happens: Article text is treated as untrusted data and never followed. Social posts are dropped. Long articles are cut at 6,000 characters.
E3 Collect and clean
What can go wrong: The same deal appears in several outlets, amounts disagree, or no stage is stated
What happens: Duplicates are merged only when amount, investor and name agree. Disagreeing amounts use the middle value, marked low confidence. A missing stage shows "Stage not reported", never a guess.
E4 Extract
What can go wrong: The AI call fails, hits a quota, or the run is cut short
What happens: Retries with a backup key. Anything unread is kept and the page says "some articles are still waiting"; pressing Refresh continues.
E5 Match and compute
What can go wrong: A company name nearly matches a Portfolio name, an amount is undisclosed, or history is too thin
What happens: Near-misses go to a Needs review list and are not counted. Undisclosed amounts are counted separately, never added. "New investor" claims are suppressed without enough history.
E6 Checks
What can go wrong: The AI writes a number that is not in the data, is too long, or cites no source
What happens: The text is rejected and retried once, then replaced by a template written from the numbers. A banner says which was used.
E7 Scheduled or on demand
What can go wrong: Refresh is pressed twice, or a run is too long for the 5-minute limit
What happens: One run at a time and a 30-minute cooldown. Each step saves its progress, so a long run can resume.
E8 Monday digest email
What can go wrong: The scheduler fires twice, or nobody is listed as a recipient
What happens: The email is claimed once per week, so it cannot repeat. With no recipients it is skipped and logged. Refresh never sends it.
E9 Sends a draft / Ask and rewrites
What can go wrong: A rewrite changes a figure or link, a demo contact is used, or too many sends
What happens: The rewrite is rejected. Demo contacts cannot be emailed. Sending needs a confirm step, an hourly and daily limit, and every send is logged.
E10 Stored digest
What can go wrong: A name the fund does not want shown appears, or a week has barely started
What happens: One shared filter hides the name everywhere, including what is sent to the AI. A week gets no digest until it is the current digest week.
E11 Builds action drafts
What can go wrong: A founder contact or LinkedIn page cannot be found
What happens: It says "Not found" and shows only what a public page or search result states, with its source. LinkedIn is a search link only.

How it is shown to work

  • 169 automated tests pass; the result is recorded and shown on the Method page.
  • Method page shows live examples of each edge case, the run log of every refresh, and this map.
  • Numbers are computed in code and were cross-checked against separate database sums during testing.
  • Every claim links to its source and is labelled Reported or Possible.
  • Data-quality strip on the dashboard: sources reached, articles read, deals found, undisclosed, needs review, last updated.
  • Tested end to end in a real browser on the live site, on desktop and phone, in light and dark themes.
  • Scheduled Monday run tested through cron-job.org.

Where it runs

  • Website and functions: Vercel
  • Database: Neon Postgres
  • AI: Gemini Flash only
  • News search: Tavily, Exa, Serper
  • Email: Resend
  • Scheduler: cron-job.org
